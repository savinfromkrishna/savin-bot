---
name: trend-research
description: Find what is working now in a niche, with evidence, and turn it into post ideas.
---

# Sources, in order

1. YouTube search suggestions and "Sort by: upload date" for the niche's core keywords.
2. The top 5 competitor channels: their last 20 uploads, sorted by views relative to channel size.
3. TikTok and Instagram Reels search for the same keywords (look at view counts in the grid).
4. Google Trends (last 7 days) for the keywords and two rising related queries.

# Output

Save `research/ideas-<date>.md`. For each idea: the angle in one line, why now (with numbers and links), the format (Short, long-form, carousel), and a draft hook. Rank by strength of evidence and keep the 3–5 best.
