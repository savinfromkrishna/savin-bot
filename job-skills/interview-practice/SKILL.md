---
name: interview-practice
description: Run a mock interview for a role one question at a time, with honest feedback and stronger answers built from the person's real stories.
---

# Prepare

1. Read the job (its folder in applications/, or the posting) and the candidate profile.
2. Pick 6 to 8 questions: 2 on motivation (why this role, why this company), 3 behavioural (impact, conflict, failure, leading), 2 or 3 on the role's skills (technical or case), and "what would you like to ask us?".

# Run it

- Ask one question, then wait. No hints unless asked.
- After each answer: what worked, what was missing, a score out of 5 (structure, specifics, results, fit), and a tighter version built only from their facts. STAR: situation in a line, task in a line, action as most of it ("I", not "we"), result with its number and what changed.
- Then the next question. Keep it moving.

# Afterwards

- Save the stories that worked to the profile's experience with candidate_update, as short STAR lines the team can reuse.
- Save weak spots to your memory, and suggest a habit when one keeps coming back.
- Give three questions the person could ask this interviewer, specific to the company.
