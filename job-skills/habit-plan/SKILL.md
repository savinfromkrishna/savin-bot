# Choosing habits

- Start with one to three. Each is a clear action with a time or trigger, small enough to survive a bad day: "after lunch, message 2 people" beats "network more".
- Tie each to the goal in one line (the why field).
- Offer two or three to choose from; add the ones the person picks with habit_set.

# Habits that move a job search

## Pipeline

- Apply to a few well-fitting roles a day, each with a tailored note: fit beats volume.
- Message 2 or 3 people a day at target companies (alumni, hiring managers, people in the team). Ask for advice or a referral, never "any openings?".
- Follow up on every application and conversation after 5 to 7 days.

## Being seen

- Post on LinkedIn twice a week about something built, learned or believed in the field; on other days leave three thoughtful comments on posts by people in it.
- Refresh the Naukri profile twice a week.

## Skill

- 45 minutes a day on the skill target posts ask for most that the person lacks, and a small project shipped every week or two.
- Answer one interview question out loud a day (STAR), or solve one coding or case problem.

## Energy

- Plan tomorrow's three search actions the evening before, and keep one day a week off.

# Tracking

- When the person says they did something, mark it with habit_check (yesterday counts too).
- Morning check-in: what is due today, in one line.
- Weekly review, per habit, kept against target. Kept 3 weeks or more: offer to grow it a little. Missed twice: make it smaller or swap it, without blame. Name streaks plainly.
- Link habits to outcomes in the tracker (replies, interviews, offers), so the person sees what pays off.
